The World Stops

Venture 1, 10:30 AM on the MIT road. Dhanasree dances, and for Ragul time stops. Across the campus, two others feel it too. One minute, rendered live in your browser.

Building the scene

How this was made

  • Everything is built and animated by code: the road, the trees, the crowd and the four of them. No models, no video.
  • Real-time lighting, depth of field, bloom and a colour grade, cut as ten shots.
  • Original music, synthesised live: a kuthu-style 3-3-2 groove that stops into one long note.
  • The words are Venture 1's, in English or the original Tanglish.
